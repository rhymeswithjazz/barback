## Fixed in Barback 0.2.1

- **Single-color drawer icons now work.** In 0.2.0 Barback never found the menu bar to capture, so every item kept its app icon. Items already in the drawer still need to be dragged out to the menu bar and back in once to pick up their menu bar icon.
- Items Barback hides are no longer captured while they are being switched off, which could give them a «« icon instead of their own.
