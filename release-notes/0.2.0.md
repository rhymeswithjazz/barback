## New in Barback 0.2.0

- **Drawer background.** Choose what the drawer is drawn on in Settings › Drawer: Menu (as before), Liquid Glass, Clear Glass, or Solid.
- **Single-color drawer icons.** Settings › Drawer › Icons can show each item as it looks in the menu bar, in one color, instead of its full-color app icon. This needs Screen Recording, which Barback asks for when you choose it, and only ever uses to capture the menu bar. An item Barback hasn't seen in the menu bar yet keeps its app icon; drag it out of the drawer and back in to pick up its menu bar icon.
